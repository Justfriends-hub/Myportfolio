§1 Unboxing

Before First Use

Congratulations on locating a fully assembled Lawal Emmanuel. No setup is required — this unit arrived already opinionated about software, and it will not be recalibrated on that point.

It ships convinced that most apps are just weather with better manners: systems that move people, money, and conversation around, and rarely admit that's the whole job. It builds the parts that move things — chat that behaves like a room, checkout that behaves like a market stall, a commute that behaves like a membership instead of a negotiation.

A secondary module handles AI-assisted motion design, and will engage without being asked.
